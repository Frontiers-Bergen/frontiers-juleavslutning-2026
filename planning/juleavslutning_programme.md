# Juleavslutning programme (draft proposal)

Draft for the organisers to react to; nothing here is decided. Times assume an evening of about 3.5 hours.
Success is the people at the juleavslutning having fun (see the Why in README.md); every app moment below serves that.

| Time | What happens | The app on the big screen and on phones |
|---|---|---|
| 17:30 | Doors open, people arrive and get a drink | Welcome slide with the invite QR code. *If built:* avatar designer, avatars walk onto the screen as people join |
| 18:00 | Welcome and a short look back at the Frontiers year | Slides; emoji smashing is open the whole evening. *If built:* fidget clicker → snowfall behind the slides |
| 18:20 | Dinner | *If built:* a background game people play at their own pace (decorate the tree, Christmas pixel canvas) |
| 19:15 | *Optional:* Frontiers awards ("talk of the year", "best demo fail") | Poll per award, results revealed one by one |
| 19:30 | **Main event: ideas for next year** | Idea bubbles: everyone adds ideas; votes blow air into bubbles; the top three burst into confetti |
| 20:00 | Retrospective: what went well, what to improve | Retro board with sticky notes |
| 20:20 | *Optional:* year-in-review quiz, then mingling | Quiz with scores and leaderboard |
| 21:00 | Thanks and goodbye | End slide. Afterwards: back up `presentation.db`, ideas and feedback are analysed from it |

## Open questions for the organisers

1. Date, venue and start time? (Sound: Gerhard has a portable system.)
2. How long should the evening be, and when is dinner served?
3. Who presents the look back at the year, and who facilitates the ideas session and the retro?
4. Which optional parts do we want: awards, quiz?
5. Will anyone join remotely, or is it in person only? (Remote viewers need the remote invite link.)

## What the app must have for this programme

Must have: welcome slide with QR code (done), emoji reactions (done), idea bubbles, retro board.
Nice to have, good workshop material: avatar designer, background dinner game, snowfall clicker, awards, quiz.
