# Good first issues for the workshop

Created as GitHub issues with the `good first issue` label (8 November 2026). Each describes a minimal playable
version a pair can finish in an evening, where to start, and stretch ideas; `MINI_GAMES.md` is the guide.

- #41 Smash milestones: celebrate fun numbers on the big screen
- #70 Reindeer race: smash the button to win the race
- #71 Decorate the tree together from your phones
- #72 Snowball fight: throw snowballs at the other team
- #73 Avatar builder: your own little avatar on the big screen; other games may use it, but none depend on it

Earlier drafts (a Christmas tree reaction, a countdown slide, a fun fact slide, Christmas colours, fastest thumb,
a result drumroll, an advent calendar, a tug of war tally) were left out on purpose: the mini-games are what
the evening is for.
