# How to contribute

Illustration: /static/illustrations/green-grump.svg

- **Complexity is the enemy:** KISS 💋 keep it simple s*
- **Cohesion:** what changes together stays together; your feature keeps its files side by side
- **Low coupling:** your change should not break anyone else's
- **Pull requests:** one PR per feature; use stacked PRs when needed
- **Merge conflicts will happen:** pull `main` often, and ask your AI agent to help you resolve them
- **Don't be the Grinch:** there are a hundred ways to ruin the evening for everybody.

Notes:

- Avoid adding accidental complexity.
- Keep things tidy.
- One PR per feature. Use stacked PRs when they get too big.
- Merge conflicts will happen. You can go on the AI's naughty list and order them to solve them for you.
- As developers, we have tremendous power. You have access to the code and the repo. There is no way we can stop you.
