# Domain dictionary

Domain terms are words unique to this project. They are used unchanged in every part of the system: code, database, UI and documentation.
Everything that is not a domain term is written in English.

Only add a word here when it is specific to this project and has no plain English equivalent we would rather use.

## Domain terms

**Frontiers** — Frontiers Bergen, the meetup group for front-end and web developers in Bergen that owns this app. Always "Frontiers", never "Frontier".

**Juleavslutning** — Frontiers' end-of-year gathering: an in-person celebration where we look back on the year and add ideas for the next one.
Better food than a usual meetup, and usually beer. Around 20–40 attendees. The app exists to make this evening fun.
In code: `juleavslutning` (lowercase in identifiers, e.g. `juleavslutningProgramme`).

## Avoided terms

**Julebord** — do not use. In Norway it implies a specific kind of event, with traditional food and a dress code, which is not what this is. Use juleavslutning.
